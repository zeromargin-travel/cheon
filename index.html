04 / THE ARTIST

Cheon Minkyeong
千 民 京

Minkyeong Cheon (千民京) is a guitarist, composer, and music producer. Her musical world bridges contemporary jazz, acoustic storytelling, and cross-border ensemble dialogues.

Through collaborative projects like The Four Winds, the Reuben Project, and duo recordings under Elea Music, she creates soundscapes of intimate, spontaneous clarity.
