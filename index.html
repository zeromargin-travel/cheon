About

Music as a living,
breathing conversation.

Minkyeong Cheon (千民京) is a guitarist, composer, and music producer whose work transcends genre boundaries. Drawing from jazz, contemporary improvisation, and world music, she crafts soundscapes that feel both deeply personal and universally resonant.

As the creative force behind projects including Elea Music, the Reuben Project, and The Four Winds, she collaborates with musicians across borders, producing recordings that capture the spontaneous magic of live musical dialogue.
